Important: Kiwi Payroll may collect location information, including background location information, when the relevant functionality is enabled and while an employee is checked in or otherwise actively using location-based attendance features.

3. Location and GPS Information

Kiwi Payroll may use your device's location services for attendance and workforce-management features.

Depending on your organization's configuration, location information may be collected to:

  • Verify attendance check-in and check-out.
  • Verify whether an employee is within an authorized work location.
  • Support geofencing-based attendance.
  • Record attendance-related location information.
  • Allow authorized managers or administrators to view employee location while the employee is actively checked in or being tracked under the organization's configured policy.
  • Support workforce and attendance management.

Background Location

Some Kiwi Payroll configurations may use background location services. When enabled, the App may collect location information while an employee is checked in or actively participating in a location-based attendance or workforce-tracking feature, even when the App is not displayed on the screen.

Background location is not intended to be used continuously when an employee is not participating in an applicable attendance or tracking session.

The exact frequency and availability of location collection may depend on the device, operating system, network conditions, application configuration, and organization settings.

You can manage location permissions through your device settings. Disabling required location permissions may prevent certain attendance or location-based features from functioning.

4. Employee Photos and Selfies

Kiwi Payroll may allow employees to upload or capture photographs for their employee profile, identity verification, or attendance verification.

Attendance selfies may be captured when required by the organization's attendance policy.

Photographs may be stored and associated with the relevant employee or attendance record so that authorized users can verify attendance and identity.

Employees should only upload photographs that they are authorized to provide.

5. Face Verification

Kiwi Payroll may provide face verification functionality to help verify that the person attempting to record attendance corresponds to the employee's registered reference photograph.

Where face verification is enabled, the App may process an employee's reference photograph and attendance photograph for identity verification.

Face verification information is used for attendance and identity verification purposes and is not intended to be used for advertising or unrelated profiling.

Organizations are responsible for enabling face verification in accordance with applicable laws, workplace policies, and required employee notices or consents.

6. Payroll Information

Kiwi Payroll may process employee payroll information provided by an employer or authorized administrator.

This may include:

  • Salary and compensation information.
  • Earnings and allowances.
  • Deductions.
  • Attendance and payable days.
  • Leave information.
  • Payroll periods.
  • Payslip information.
  • Other information required for payroll administration.

Payroll information is used to calculate, manage, report, and administer employee compensation and related records.
